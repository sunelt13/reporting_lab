    Build Your Weekly Report with Copilot Cowork

    From MSXi to a trusted, explainable weekly readout — in one working session.

    • ~45 min in the room + 5 min pre-lab
    • 5 exercises
    • 14 steps
    • Cowork steps checked 3 Oct 2026
    Start with pre-lab setup ↓

    What you'll leave with

    1. A Cowork recipe that builds this week's version of your report from MSXi — with every filter it applied shown on the page.
    2. The context your leadership asks for: what changed, what's at risk, what's stale.
    3. A scheduled weekly run — and your recipe written down, which is what SE&O needs to build the durable version.
    Ground rules
    • You pull your own numbers under your own access. Nothing leaves your session unless you choose to share it.
    • Cowork reads the same certified MSXi model you already use. It is not a different source of truth.
    • Expect about 80% on the first pass. The 20% gap is the point of today: it is your definitions, written down.
    How the session starts

    Your facilitator opens with a 5-minute demo — one pre-built task on their own numbers, ending in an HTML page with a "Filters applied" box. Then you build the same thing with yours.

    Required

    Pre-lab setup

    Write your recipe

    8 min2 steps0 of 2 done

    1

    Fill in your Recipe Card

    Exercise 1 · Step 1 of 2

    Write it the way you'd explain it to a smart intern on their first day. Copy this block into Notepad or straight into Cowork and fill in the brackets.

    Recipe Card
    RECIPE CARD
1. Report and page I use: [MSXi report name] → [page/tab]
2. Slicers / filters I set, with their values: [e.g., Segment = …, Solution Area = …, Fiscal period = …]
3. What's on my slide — each bar or number and what it measures: [e.g., "Qualified pipeline $ per product for (my org's name)"]
4. Time window / as-of: [current quarter? fiscal YTD? as of Friday close?] Live MSXi or a weekly snapshot? [live / snapshot]
5. What I do to the numbers AFTER I pull them: [combine pages or reports? roll up to my org? exclude anything? compare to target? week-over-week vs last slide? Excel in between?]
6. Judgment calls I make by hand: [items I drop or adjust, rounding, anything "you'd just know"]
7. What the final output looks like and who gets it: [one slide, bar chart by product, goes to …]
8. Anything on my slide that is NOT from MSXi: [e.g., a target from a spreadsheet, a manual note, another system — or "nothing"]
    Tip

    Items 5 and 6 matter most. If you'd say "you'll just know which ones to leave out" — write exactly that. It's the part no dashboard can see.

    Two more that save time
    • Line 8 heads off a false alarm. Cowork pulls only from MSXi, so anything listed there won't appear unless you attach it.
    • Name your org the way MSXi labels it — not "my team".

    You're done when: all eight lines have something in them, even if it's "nothing".

    Paste the Build prompt, with your Recipe Card in the middle

    Exercise 2 · Step 3 of 6

    Copy the prompt, paste your filled-in Recipe Card where it says to, and send it.

    Build prompt
    I need this week's version of my weekly business report. Last week's version is attached — use it for the layout and for week-over-week comparison, NOT as the numbers to hit. Pull everything fresh from the MSX Insights (MSXi) Power BI report, as me.

Here is how I'd explain my report to a new intern:
[PASTE YOUR RECIPE CARD HERE]

Before you pull anything, ask me any clarifying questions — don't guess.
Then produce an HTML page with:
- the numbers, in the same layout as my slide
- a "Filters applied" box listing every filter, scope rule and transformation you used
- a short list of anything you had to assume
Double-check your figures and walk me through your reasoning.
    Important

    Do not tell it the number you expect. Let it show its work — that's how you'll see where a definition differs from yours.

    You're done when: the prompt is sent.

    Next: 2.4 Answer its questions →
    4

    Answer its questions, then let it run

    Exercise 2 · Step 4 of 6

    It will ask one or two things. Answer in plain English. If it shows choices, pick one and select Submit — don't select Skip, which tells it to carry on and guess.

    Then it runs for 2–5 minutes. While you wait, get your three checks ready:

    • ShapeSame metrics, same cuts, same layout as my slide?
    • Locked numbersAnything already closed (prior months, actuals, targets) should match last week's slide exactly.
    • Live numbersAnything still moving (open pipeline, forecast) should match the MSXi window right now. If your card says weekly snapshot, match that snapshot instead.

    You're done when: the page appears.

    Next: 2.5 Score the result →
    5

    Score the result

    Exercise 2 · Step 5 of 6

    Run the three checks. Note what matched and what didn't.

    Leave out anything you listed on line 8 of your card. Cowork pulls only from MSXi, so those aren't misses.

    {{Screenshot: example output page with "Filters applied" box highlighted}}
    Match or mismatch — both are wins

    A match means your recipe is right and repeatable. A mismatch means you just found a definition gap, in your own words. Both are wins — write down which one you got.

    You're done when: each of the three checks has a yes or no.

    Next: 2.6 If something doesn't match →

    If something doesn't match, ask it to find the gap

    Exercise 2 · Step 6 of 6

    Fill in the brackets and send it.

    Gap prompt
    Your figure for [ITEM] is [its number]. MSXi with my slicers shows [MSXi number] right now. Don't just change the number — find the filter, scope or definition difference that explains the gap, tell me what it is in one sentence, then rebuild with the corrected definition and show the updated "Filters applied" box.

    Do this once or twice. If it's still off, raise your hand — that's a finding for SE&O, not a failure.

    You're done when: it matches, or you've captured the reason it doesn't.
